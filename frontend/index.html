      How to read the AQI

      The US AQI summarizes air pollution on a single scale. A higher number indicates greater health concern.

      Why US AQI? US AQI is the air-quality scale we use to compare pollution levels in Pakistani cities.

      US AQI guide from AirNow

      About the data

      Anemos uses the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) global model through Open-Meteo. Its grid spacing is about 45 km. Pollution can vary from street to street within that area.

      “Estimate valid at” shows the time the values describe, in Pakistan Standard Time (PKT). Model runs and data retrieval have their own times.

      PM2.5 and PM10 describe particles up to about 2.5 and 10 micrometres across. The unit µg/m³ is micrograms of particles per cubic metre of air.

      Data from Open-Meteo and the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
